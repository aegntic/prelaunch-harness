# Surfaces

Walk in this order. A surface not seen is unknown.

1. Public pages and app shells. Fonts, scripts, pixels, replay.
2. Account creation and any form that stores an email or name.
3. Mail templates and the provider config. Marketing versus transactional.
4. Checkout and the customer portal. Recurring price, cadence, cancel.
5. Upload and share paths. Who can post a file another person did not make.
6. Mobile shells. Webviews pull the same third parties as the site.
7. Agent or generated clients. A generated fetch to a CDN counts.

Record for each surface: present, absent, or unknown. Do not collapse unknown into absent.
